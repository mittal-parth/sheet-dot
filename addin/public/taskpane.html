Workbook compatibility

Try =AI.ASK("Which NASA mission first landed humans on the Moon? Use web search."). Pass a range as the second argument to include its values.

WATCH supports "every 1 min", "daily 8am", and "in 1 min". Select a recurring WATCH cell and stop its schedule before removing or moving it.

In empty cells, try =AI.PROBE(), =AI.PROBEARRAY(), and =AI.PROBESTREAM(). Expect text, a two-by-two grid, and a change from Working to Ready.
